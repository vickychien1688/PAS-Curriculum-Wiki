## Cultural goal
Children discover that familiar car brands come from Germany. Logo matching is a visual tool, not a brand-memory, reading, or spelling test.

## Starter Logo Hunt
Title: Find the German Cars!
Closing text printed on worksheet: These cars are from Germany!
One worksheet contains ten brands in mixed order; one corresponding exploration card per brand, with real vehicle photograph, authentic brand logo, national flag and country name.

Germany: Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Porsche, Volkswagen.
Japan: Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Mazda.
United States: Tesla.

Country labels describe historical brand origin/cultural identity, not the photographed vehicle's manufacturing location, current ownership, or first sales market. Lexus was developed by Japan's Toyota and launched first in the US. Porsche's company roots are in Stuttgart; its first branded sports car was made in Austria.

Children visit cards and circle the five German brands using flag and country-name evidence. All cards share one style. The worksheet has no pre-circled answer. Teacher demonstrates BMW, explicitly counts it as one of five, then asks children to find four more. Compare Toyota/Japan and leave it uncircled.

## Differentiated language
Starter teacher: Look! A BMW! / From Germany!
Starter child: points and circles; repeats Germany! / From Germany! No reading or spelling requirement.
