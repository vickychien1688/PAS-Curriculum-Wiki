# Germany Project — Pilot

> Country Culture Project Pilot 01
>
> Status: Planning / framework approved; detailed level-language alignment and resource production still to be completed.

> Framework: [Country Culture Project Framework](country-culture-project-framework.md)

## 1. Pilot Definition

Germany is the first PAS Country Culture Project pilot.

The purpose is twofold:
1. give students a memorable five-week introduction to Germany inside their regular English learning;
2. validate a reusable production and teaching framework that can later scale to other countries.

## 3. Five-Week Cultural Curriculum Overview

### Week 1 — Meet Germany 🇩🇪

**Theme:** Country & People

**What we hope students learn:**
Students know that Germany is a country in Europe and begin to recognize its national identity, including the German flag, Berlin, the German language, and a simple greeting such as `Hallo!`.

**Optional teacher directions:**
Map, flag, Berlin, greeting, simple Germany/Taiwan connections or comparisons appropriate to level.

### Week 2 — Magical Germany 🏰📖

**Theme:** Castles & Fairy Tales

**What we hope students learn:**
Students recognize Germany's connection with famous castles and understand that the Grimm Brothers and many familiar fairy tales are part of German cultural history.

**Optional teacher directions:**
Neuschwanstein Castle, Grimm Brothers, familiar story characters, Guess the Fairy Tale, picture/story discussion.

### Week 3 — Made in Germany 🚗💡

**Theme:** Cars, Ideas & Inventions

**What we hope students learn:**
Students understand that Germany is well known for engineering, design, automobiles, and important ideas/inventions, building the broader concept that Germany has a strong culture of making and designing things.

**Optional teacher directions:**
German car brands/logos, Autobahn, one or two age-appropriate inventions, engineering/design examples.

### Week 4 — Life in Germany 🎵⚽

**Theme:** Music, Sports & Daily Life

**What we hope students learn:**
Students see Germany as a living culture by exploring selected examples of music, sports, cities, school, leisure, or daily life and connecting them with their own experiences.

**Optional teacher directions:**
Beethoven, football, cities, school/daily-life images, simple Germany/Taiwan comparison.

### Week 5 — Taste & Experience Germany 🥨🇩🇪

**Theme:** Food & Cultural Experience

**What we hope students learn:**
Students recognize selected representative German foods and use the final experience to retrieve and connect what they have learned about Germany during the previous four weeks.

**Main event direction:**
Germany Day / German market-style experience with food tasting where feasible. The event should recycle Weeks 1–4 rather than become a sixth unrelated topic.

Possible foods may include pretzel/Brezel, bread, sausage, potato dishes, or cake. Final selections depend on feasibility, safety, budget, and event planning.

## 4. Language Principle — Explicit Spiral Review

The 10 regular lessons occur during EOW Unit 1, but Germany Project language is **not restricted to the exact Unit 1 grammar currently being introduced**.

Teachers/resources may intentionally retrieve language students learned previously when it fits the Germany context naturally.

Use:

> **Current Learning + Prior Learning + Spiral Review**

Examples of differentiation philosophy:
- Starter / early levels: small set of accessible vocabulary and familiar oral patterns
- Middle levels: sentence building, Q&A, description, guided comprehension
- Higher levels: short reading, comparison, explanation, opinion, discussion, and transfer

Exact patterns must be selected from PAS curriculum sources before production. Do not invent an alignment when source data is missing.

## 5. Teacher Communication Principle

Teachers should receive the weekly **goal**, not a rigid script.

> **Same Goal, Different Ways to Learn.**

Teachers may use their own approach and may also create their own materials.

PAS will progressively publish optional resources aligned to each week's goal. Resources may include PDF, Word, PPT/slides, short videos/clips, images, and activity suggestions.

Teachers can use resources as provided, adapt them, combine them, or create their own.

## 6. Production Order — Do Not Skip

Before generating final Germany worksheets/resources:
1. Approve this five-week cultural map.
2. Read PAS curriculum sources for Starter and Levels 1–5.
3. Build the Germany Level Language Alignment Map using current + prior learning.
4. Decide the minimum useful resource pack for each week.
5. Produce resources.
6. Design the Week 5 Germany Day in detail.
7. Pilot with teachers/students.
8. Record post-pilot findings and update the reusable framework.
