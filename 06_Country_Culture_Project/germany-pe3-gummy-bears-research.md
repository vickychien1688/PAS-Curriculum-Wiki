## Topic and verified cultural background

The teachers described gummy bears as originating in Germany. The user withdrew certainty about the green animated Gummibär character and requested fact-checking. HARIBO’s official history identifies Hans Riegel’s company founding in Bonn in 1920 and the Dancing Bear fruit gummy in 1922. The September 24 curriculum draft also concerns edible HARIBO candy; it does not establish a lesson about the musical character.

Distinguish the company-founding year from the candy’s year, the earlier shape from modern Goldbears, and historical origin from a current package’s manufacturing country. Hans and Riegel are the given and family names of one founder; Bonn is the founding city. The brand acronym combines parts of that name and city. Do not assign fixed flavors from colors.

Sources: [HARIBO official history](https://www.haribo.com/en/about-us/history), [Goldbears official product page](https://www.haribo.com/en/products/haribo/goldbears). The separate character was checked against [the designer’s portfolio](https://petedodd.com/portfolio_page/gummy-bear/).
