- If using 14,125 islands, attribute the 2023 GSI count and its criteria; do not imply newly created islands. [GSI source](https://www.gsi.go.jp/kihonjohochousa/pressrelease20230228).
- Treat Fuji as an active volcano under JMA's classification. Do not present active/dormant as mutually exclusive modern categories. Omit an unqualified fixed annual earthquake count. [JMA definition](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/know/kazan/katsukazan_toha/katsukazan_toha.html).
- Separate preparation, protection during shaking and subsequent school-directed action. Use the classroom-specific guidance. [CWA/NFA guide](https://scweb.cwa.gov.tw/webdata/PDF/Earthquake_precautions/).
