## Accuracy and implementation constraints

- If using 14,125 islands, attribute the 2023 GSI count and its criteria; do not imply newly created islands. [GSI source](https://www.gsi.go.jp/kihonjohochousa/pressrelease20230228).
- Treat Fuji as an active volcano under JMA's classification. Do not present active/dormant as mutually exclusive modern categories. Omit an unqualified fixed annual earthquake count. [JMA definition](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/know/kazan/katsukazan_toha/katsukazan_toha.html).
- Separate preparation, protection during shaking and subsequent school-directed action. Use the classroom-specific guidance. [CWA/NFA guide](https://scweb.cwa.gov.tw/webdata/PDF/Earthquake_precautions/).
- Preserve the user's nuanced instant-noodle account. Corporate product dates do not settle all questions of prior invention; the Nissin patent-history page anonymizes another firm and alone does not identify Chang Kuo-wen. [Nissin history](https://www.nissin.com/jp/about/history/columns/1630).
- The QR diagram is explanatory, not scannable. Functional classroom codes must be generated and tested separately; generated art is not a real code. Distinguish public specifications and patent non-enforcement from all services being free. [DENSO WAVE](https://www.qrcode.com/en/history/).
- Distinguish emoji from text emoticons and machine interoperability from human interpretation. [Unicode](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr51/).
