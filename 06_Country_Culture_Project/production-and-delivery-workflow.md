# Culture material production and delivery workflow

Updated 2026-09-29. The complete reusable workflow lives in private claude-config under `skills/culture-worksheet/references/production-workflow.md`, linked from both culture-worksheet and culture-audio. This public file records the curriculum production standard, not the proprietary materials.

1. Fetch the current configuration and curriculum repositories; read WORKFLOW, inventory, handoff, approved source files and real prior worksheets. Preserve existing user decisions.
2. Match content to the actual level/unit, keep culture articles concise, verify history and illustrations, and align visual tasks, answers and audio order.
3. Use actual PDF 13 pt for future culture reading passages, activity clues and choices; activity instructions/column headings may be 12 pt. Retain natural paragraph flow, title hierarchy and compact consistent section spacing (approved PE2 example: 8 pt). Do not rewrite content to fit. Reuse the approved series identity and production pipeline. Keep text and QR vector-based; apply the common PE3 listening component before publishing, then inspect the final page and details.
4. Deliver playable sentence recordings and, where specified, continuous Whole Story audio. PE3 L3 and L4/L5 use Whole Story as the last list item, labelled only by that name; do not insert three-second practice gaps. Preserve exact approved files and lesson-specific voice settings.
5. Publish at least one corresponding representative image above the audio list; verify real loading and playback, then decode the final worksheet QR. PE3 L4/L5 share sp/101 and one approved recording set. Physical phone scanning is a separate check.
6. PE2 Level 4/5 delivery: Desktop PDF/PNG/JPG; franchise library retains the existing PDF/PNG pairs; Drive retains three original PDFs per level with updated versions. PE3 delivery: Desktop PDF/PNG/JPG; franchise library PDF/JPG under each level's Holiday/Germany Journey/Project3; Drive only one final PDF per worksheet, updated in place with the original ID. Verify actual destination files, not only upload notifications.
7. Update handoff, asset index, version/source/hash evidence and outstanding checks. Store full materials privately and curriculum specifications publicly; push and verify remote commits.

The Starter raster-body/series-size gap remains separate from the completed delivery. Updating skill documentation does not constitute fixing that worksheet issue or reapproving the audio.
