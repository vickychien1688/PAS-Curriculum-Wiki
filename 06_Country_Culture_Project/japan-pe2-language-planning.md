## Cultural scope and activity direction

- Anime: retain Studio Ghibli / My Neighbor Totoro, Doraemon, Detective Conan and Pokémon in the teacher's introduction cards. Lower-level paragraphs focus on fewer names. Identify Ghibli as a studio, not a character. Pokémon spans animation and games; these are not mutually exclusive categories.
- Nintendo: use Mario and Luigi's official colors, brother relationship and relative height. Activities can use character cards; electronic gameplay is teacher-selected, not a requirement. Do not infer height from independently resized pictures.
- Capsule toys: recognise the machine, coin and handle; sequence operation; understand that the exact toy is unknown before dispensing. Teachers may use a machine or clearly labelled classroom capsule simulation. All groups can reuse materials, with one turn each and different observation prompts. Gashapon is a BANDAI brand; capsule toy is the generic term. Do not describe the original machine as a Japanese invention.
