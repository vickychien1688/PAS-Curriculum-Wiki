## Family, food and clothing

Food is a shared family outing with both parents, a boy and a girl. One table contains eight different dishes, one plate or portion each, introduced individually with varied family interactions: bratwurst, roast pork knuckle, roast chicken, pretzel, sauerkraut, potato pancake, apple strudel and Sachertorte. Sachertorte is explicitly described as Austrian; availability at the festival was checked against the Café Theres menu.

The mother wears a plausible modest Dirndl blouse with a shallow square neckline, broad opaque white panel above the bust, comfortable natural fit, blue floral dress and plain blue waist apron. Visible cleavage, exaggerated bust, arbitrary ordinary shirt collars and earlier rejected clothing variants are excluded. Clothing is Bavarian regional traditional dress, not a single national German uniform. Dirndl, apron and Lederhosen are directly identified with labels and arrows.

## Source checking

Research included the user's [DW Travel film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eom1s9LRKik), actual clothing examples, official [Dirndl guidance](https://www.oktoberfest.de/en/attractions/tradition/perfect-dirndl-oktoberfest), Munich Tourism and actual BR opening footage. The opening shows a mallet tapping the outer end of the horizontal spigot, followed by a separate lively cheering crowd; it is an illustrative reconstruction rather than documentary footage. See the official [opening ceremony description](https://www.oktoberfest.de/en/information/events/oktoberfest-tapping-and-opening-ceremony).

Historical sources include the official [festival history](https://www.oktoberfest.de/en/magazine/tradition/the-history-of-oktoberfest) and [Historical Lexicon of Bavaria](https://www.historisches-lexikon-bayerns.de/Lexikon/EN:Oktoberfest). The wedding occurred on October 12, 1810, followed by the horse race on October 17. Child-facing narration avoids falsely assigning sole organization to either the public or the royal family. Calendar graphics distinguish Start in September and End in October; 9 and 10 are months.

The separate cooking sequence covers adult-led peeling, grating, squeezing, mixing, frying both sides, serving with applesauce and cooling before eating. Technique was checked against BDA demonstrations; the recipe was checked against [LURCH](https://www.lurch.de/recipes/Rezepte/Kartoffelpuffer-Grundrezept) and ORF. It is an abbreviated animated teaching sequence, not a representation of actual cooking duration. Detailed recipe, sources and adult-tool guidance remain in the private teacher note.
