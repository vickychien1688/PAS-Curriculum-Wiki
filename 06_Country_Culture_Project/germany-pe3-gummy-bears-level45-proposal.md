# Gummy Bears Level 4/5 Shared Content Proposal

**Status: content proposal for review.** No passage, activity, guide, worksheet layout, audio, or publication is approved. Exact student-facing copy and choices remain in the private work package.

Level 4/5 advances from Level 3’s gummy-bear origin story to how the early Dancing Bear’s shape changed into today’s Goldbear. The proposed task asks pupils to compare the authentic early bear and a current Goldbear image, then select two text-supported shape clues. A short German-name note uses *Tanzbär*.

The draft assumes Level 4 and Level 5 share the content and task. The prior handoff records Unit 1 complete and Unit 2 not yet taught for both levels. Available EOW4 Unit 1 scope lists did questions and short answers; a current EOW5 scope file was not available in this checkout. Historical narration and comparative vocabulary therefore remain teacher-supported.

The official HARIBO history supports the 1922 Dancing Bear, its larger/slimmer shape, Goldbears reaching the market in 1960, and the 1978 change to shorter feet and a more compact shape. The official German history calls the early bear *Tanzbär*.

Sources: [HARIBO official English history](https://www.haribo.com/en/about-us/history), [HARIBO official German history](https://www.haribo.com/de-de/ueber-uns/geschichte).
