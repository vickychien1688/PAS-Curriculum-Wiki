## Unit 1

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Be Verb: Is** | `Is it a pen?` · `Is it an eraser?` · `Is it paper?` · `Yes, it is.` · `No, it isn’t.` · `It’s a pen/an eraser.` · `It’s paper.` | `it isn’t = it is not = it’s not` | Answer from dialogue; tick the correct Yes／No answer; write a sentence beginning with `It’s`. |
| **2. What, What Color, and How Many** | `What is it?` → `It’s a/an ...` · `What color is it?` → `It’s ...` · `How many markers?` → `Two. / There are two.` | Full question: `How many markers are in the box?` | Answer from dialogue; unscramble and answer a question; write a question for a given answer. |

**Generation boundary:** Unit 1 grammar must support identifying classroom objects, asking their colors, and asking their numbers. Do not replace these targets with unrelated sentence patterns.

## Unit 2

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Be Verbs: Is and Are** | `What is it?` → `It’s a bird/the moon.` · `What are they?` → `They’re butterflies.` | `is` is used for one thing／`it`; `are` is used for more than one thing／`they`. `It’s = It is`; `They’re = They are`. | Answer `What is it?`／`What are they?`; identify the wrong singular or plural answer. |
| **2. Where** | `Where is the butterfly?` → `It’s on the flower.` · `Where are the clouds?` → `They’re in the sky.` | `It’s = It is`; `They’re = They are`. | Answer location questions; write a question for a location answer; connect nouns to `Where is ...?` or `Where are ...?`. |

## Unit 3

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Present Simple: Have** | `Do you have a brother?` · `Do you have any brothers or sisters?` · `Yes, I do.` · `No, I don’t.` · `How many brothers do you have?` · `I have two brothers.` · `I don’t have any brothers.` | Use `big/little` or `older/younger` before `brother(s)` and `sister(s)` for a clearer description. | Answer from dialogue; match family pictures to sentences; unscramble questions and statements. |
| **2. Who** | `Who’s he?` → `He’s my brother/uncle/dad.` · `Who’s she?` → `She’s my sister/aunt/mom.` · `He’s/She’s ...` + adjective | `Who’s = Who is`; `He’s = He is`; `She’s = She is`. | Identify family members; match identity and description sentences; complete family-word blanks. |

## Unit 4

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. There is …** | `Is there a table in the kitchen?` · `Is there an armchair in the kitchen?` · `Yes, there is.` · `No, there isn’t.` · `There is a table ...` · `There isn’t an armchair ...` | Singular existence with `there is`; affirmative and negative forms. | Answer Yes／No existence questions; make `There is` or `There isn’t` sentences from prompts. |
| **2. Present Continuous: Is** | `Where’s your father/mother?` → `He’s/She’s in the ...` · `What’s he/she doing?` → `He’s/She’s ...-ing.` | `Where’s = Where is`; `He’s = He is`; `She’s = She is`. | Match people to rooms and actions; answer paired location and present-continuous questions. |

## Unit 5

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Present Continuous: Are** | `What are you wearing?` → `I’m wearing ...` · `What is he/she wearing?` → `He’s/She’s wearing ...` | Use `and` to join more than one clothing item. `I am = I’m`; `He is = He’s`; `She is = She’s`. | Choose the correct be verb; answer clothing questions; draw and describe clothing. |
| **2. That and Those** | `What is that?` → `That is/That’s my ...` · `What are those?` → `Those are my ...` | `that` is singular and distant; `those` is plural and distant. | Complete dialogue blanks; match questions to pictures and answers; unscramble sentences. |

## Unit 6

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Do and Does** | `Do they/you/I/we want a puppet?` → `Yes, ... do.`／`No, ... don’t.` · `Does he/she want a puppet?` → `Yes, ... does.`／`No, ... doesn’t.` | More than one name maps to `they`; a boy’s name maps to `he`; a girl’s name maps to `she`. | Answer from dialogue; choose `do/does` and matching forms; match questions to short answers. |
| **2. Is and Are** | `Is this/that your water bottle?` → `Yes, it is.`／`No, it isn’t.` · `Are these/those new sneakers?` → `Yes, they are.`／`No, they aren’t.` | `this/these` are nearby; `that/those` are distant. `this/that` are singular; `these/those` are plural. `it` refers to `this/that`; `they` refers to `these/those`. | Fill `this/that/these/those`; choose the correct singular or plural short answer. |

## Unit 7

| Lesson | Core Patterns | Remember / Usage | Practice Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. My, Your, His, and Her** | `My/Your/His/Her hair is brown.` · `My/Your/His/Her eyes are brown.` · `I/You have ...` · `He/She has ...` | Use `its` for a thing, such as an animal. | Complete descriptions; match pictures and possessive sentences; rewrite `have/has` sentences with possessive adjectives. |
| **2. Can** | `Can you/he/she jump?` · `Yes, I/he/she can.` · `No, I/he/she can’t.` · `I/He/She can/can’t jump.` | Ability with `can`; negative form `can’t`. | Identify the wrong answer; answer ability questions from pictures. |
