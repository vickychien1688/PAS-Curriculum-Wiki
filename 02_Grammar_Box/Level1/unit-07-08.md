## Generator Guidance

Unit 7 should connect possessive adjectives to visible body features before moving to ability questions. Unit 8 must distinguish first-person `like` from third-person `likes`, and `doesn’t like` must retain the base form. Keep `a/an` practice picture-supported and limited to singular count nouns shown by the source models.
