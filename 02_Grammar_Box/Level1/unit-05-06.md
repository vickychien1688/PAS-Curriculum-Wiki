## Generator Guidance

Unit 5 should keep clothing descriptions inside the present-continuous frame and use `that/those` for distant reference. Unit 6 must distinguish `do` subjects from `does` subjects before adding `this/that/these/those` with the correct `is/are` and `it/they` answers.
