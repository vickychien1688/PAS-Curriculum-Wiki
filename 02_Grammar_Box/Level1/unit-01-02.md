## Topic Map

| EOW Unit | PDF Pages | Lesson | Verified Grammar Scope | Practice Types |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–2 | Be Verb: Is | `Is it a/an ...?`; `Yes, it is.`; `No, it isn’t.`; `It’s a/an ...`; uncountable `paper` without an article | Dialogue-based answers; tick Yes／No; write an `It’s ...` sentence from a prompt word |
| 1 | 3–4 | What, What Color, and How Many | `What is it?`; `What color is it?`; `How many ...?`; object, color, and number answers | Answer questions; unscramble questions; write a question for a given answer |
| 2 | 5–6 | Be Verbs: Is and Are | `What is it?`／`What are they?`; `It’s ...`／`They’re ...`; singular and plural reference | Picture-supported answers; identify the wrong singular or plural response |
| 2 | 7–8 | Where | `Where is ...?`／`Where are ...?`; `It’s ...`／`They’re ...`; locations with `in` and `on` | Write location questions; draw from an answer; connect nouns to `Where is` or `Where are` |

## Generator Guidance

Unit 1 materials should preserve both Lesson 1’s Yes／No identification pattern and Lesson 2’s `What`, `What color`, and `How many` targets. Unit 2 materials should distinguish `it/is` from `they/are` and keep location practice within `in` and `on` unless review is explicitly requested.
