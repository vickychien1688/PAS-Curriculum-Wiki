## Topic Map

| EOW Unit | PDF Pages | Lesson | Verified Grammar Scope | Practice Types |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 3 | 9–10 | Present Simple: Have | `Do you have ...?`; `Yes, I do.`／`No, I don’t.`; `How many ... do you have?`; `I have ...`; `I don’t have any ...` | Answer dialogue questions; match family pictures to sentences; unscramble questions and statements |

## Generator Guidance

Unit 3 should keep `have` questions and `who` identification distinct while allowing them to stack in a family description. Unit 4 should teach singular `there is` before moving to `he/she is + verb-ing`; location and action may be paired in controlled questions.
