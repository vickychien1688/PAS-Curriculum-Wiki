# EOW1 Unit 8: Good Food

## Official Scope

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Theme | Good Food |
| Goals | Name food; talk about things we like and don't like to eat; talk about my favorite food. |
| Vocabulary 1 | apple, banana, chicken, cookie, egg, fish, rice, sandwich, soup |
| Vocabulary 2 | lemonade, milk, orange juice, tea, water |
| Vocabulary Strategy | Multiple-meaning words |
| Grammar 1 | **Present Simple: Like** — `Do you like ...?`; `Does he/she like ...?`; `like/likes` and negative forms |
| Grammar 2 | **A and An** — article choice before singular food nouns |
| Reading | Fun Food |
| Reading Strategy | Summarize |
| Value | Eat good food. |

## Generation Boundaries

- Food and drink vocabulary should stay inside this unit unless marked as review.
- Use `Do you like ...?`／`Does he/she like ...?` with `like/likes` and `don’t like/doesn’t like`.
- Articles should focus on `a/an`; do not introduce advanced countability rules.
