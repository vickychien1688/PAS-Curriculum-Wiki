1. Let's say the colors. 1, 2, 3.
2. Red, green, red. Green, red, green.
3. Brown, blue, brown. Blue, brown, blue.
4. Black, yellow, black. Yellow, black, yellow.
5. Red, green, brown. Blue, black, yellow.
6. Colors are fun! Hooray! Hooray!
