## Art Rules

- Original cute flat cartoon, **vivid saturated colors**, thick dark outlines, simple background, no text or speech bubbles.
- Object colors match the vocabulary pictures (bag = red school backpack, book = blue, crayon = green, pencil = yellow with pink eraser).
- Vocabulary pictures: one subject, centered, **transparent background (PNG)**; no Chinese on vocabulary cards.
- Sentence pictures: one question + one answer per picture (differs from the textbook's single page).
- Letter cards: big upper- and lower-case letter on the left (Comic Sans, single-storey a), the unit's two sound words on the right.
