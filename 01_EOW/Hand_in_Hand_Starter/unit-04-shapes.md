# Hand in Hand Starter Unit 4: Shapes

Source: Student Book audio script (transcribed from the textbook CD, 2026-09-29) and the official Scope and Sequence. See the [scope and sequence](scope-and-sequence.md) for the one-page overview.

## Official Scope

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Theme | Shapes |
| Words | circle, square, star, triangle |
| English Sounds | /e/ egg, elephant; /f/ fish, fan |

## Conversation

- **Joey:** What color is this?
- **Mila:** It's green.
- **Joey:** Here you are.
- **Mila:** Thank you.

## Sentences (one question + one answer per picture)

- What shape is this? — It's a square.
- What shape is this? — It's a star.
- What shape is this? — It's a circle.
- What shape is this? — It's a triangle.

## Chant (line by line)

1. Let's say the shapes. 1, 2, 3.
2. Triangle, triangle, circle, circle.
3. Star, star, square, square.
4. Star, circle, triangle, square.
5. Circle, square, triangle, star.
6. Shapes are fun.
7. Hooray! Hooray! Yippee!

## English Sounds

- **E e** /e/: egg, elephant (audio: `/e/ /e/ /e/`, then `/e/ /e/ egg`, `/e/ /e/ elephant`)
- **F f** /f/: fish, fan (audio: `/f/ /f/ /f/`, then `/f/ /f/ fish`, `/f/ /f/ fan`)

### English Sounds Story

1. Look!
2. The elephant has an egg.
3. Look!
4. The elephant has a fish.
5. Look!
6. The elephant has a fan.
