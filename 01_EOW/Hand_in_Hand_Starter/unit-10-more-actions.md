## Generation Boundaries

- Use only this unit's words and sentences, plus earlier Starter units. Do not add new nouns or sentence patterns.
- Characters: Mila, Joey, Matt, Jade (see [characters](characters.md)); keep their look consistent.
- Starter learners are 5–7 years old and pre-literate: prefer listen-and-point, match, color, circle; no writing beyond tracing.
- Not used on the platform: songs, the Ditto & Fluffy comic, and the textbook listening-test track.
