## Conversation

- **Joey's mom:** Do you want a cookie?
- **Joey:** Yes, please.
- **Joey's mom:** Do you want more?
- **Joey:** No, thank you.

## Sentences (one question + one answer per picture)

- What snack do you want? — I want juice.
- What snack do you want? — I want a cookie.
- What snack do you want? — I want an apple.
- What snack do you want? — I want ice cream.
