# Hand in Hand Starter — Scope and Sequence

Source: Hand in Hand Starter textbook (official Scope and Sequence pages 30–31).

## Overview

Hand in Hand Starter is the foundational textbook used for PAS Starter-level students (first-year learners, typically ages 5–7). It covers 12 units plus 6 School Link sections (story comics and integrated activities). The curriculum introduces basic English conversation, vocabulary, sentence patterns, and English sounds (phonics awareness) through thematic units.

## Generator Rule

When generating materials for Starter level, use only the vocabulary, conversation, sentences, and English sounds assigned to the relevant Hand in Hand Starter unit(s). Cross-reference with the Summer Camp Alignment table above to determine which units apply to each project week.
