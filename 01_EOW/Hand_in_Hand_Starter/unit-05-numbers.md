# Hand in Hand Starter Unit 5: Numbers

Source: Student Book audio script (transcribed from the textbook CD, 2026-09-29) and the official Scope and Sequence. See the [scope and sequence](scope-and-sequence.md) for the one-page overview.

## Conversation

- **Store Clerk:** How old are you?
- **Mila:** I'm 6.
- **Store Clerk:** Here you are.
- **Mila:** Thank you.

## Sentences (one question + one answer per picture)

- How many triangles? — Two triangles.
- How many circles? — Four circles.
- How many stars? — Five stars.
- How many squares? — Three squares.

## Chant (line by line)

1. Let's say the numbers.
2. Ready, set, go!
3. One, two, three.
4. One, two, three. Four, five, six.
5. One, two, three. Four, five, six. Seven, eight, nine.
6. One, two, three. Four, five, six. Seven, eight, nine. Ten, eleven, twelve.
7. Yes! Yes!
8. I can count from one to twelve.

## English Sounds

- **G g** /g/: girl, gorilla (audio: `/g/ /g/ /g/`, then `/g/ /g/ girl`, `/g/ /g/ gorilla`)
- **H h** /h/: hat, hippo (audio: `/h/ /h/ /h/`, then `/h/ /h/ hat`, `/h/ /h/ hippo`)

### English Sounds Story

1. Look at the girl.
2. The girl has a hat.
3. Look at the gorilla.
4. The gorilla has a hat.
5. Look at the hippo.
6. The hippo has a hat.

## Generation Boundaries

- Use only this unit's words and sentences, plus earlier Starter units. Do not add new nouns or sentence patterns.
- Characters: Mila, Joey, Matt, Jade (see [characters](characters.md)); keep their look consistent.
- Starter learners are 5–7 years old and pre-literate: prefer listen-and-point, match, color, circle; no writing beyond tracing.
- Not used on the platform: songs, the Ditto & Fluffy comic, and the textbook listening-test track.
