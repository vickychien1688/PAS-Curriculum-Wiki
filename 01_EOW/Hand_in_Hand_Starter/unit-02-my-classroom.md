## Conversation

- What's your name?
- **Matt:** My name's Matt.
- What's your name?
- **Jade:** I'm Jade.

## Sentences (one question + one answer per picture)

- Is this a table? — Yes, it is.
- Is this a chair? — Yes, it is.
- Is this a globe? — No, it isn't. It's a board.
- Is this a board? — No, it isn't. It's a globe.

## Chant (line by line)

1. Look around the classroom.
2. What do you see?
3. Chair, chair, board, board.
4. Table, table, globe, globe.
5. Chair, table, globe, board.
6. Chair, globe, board, table.
7. Yippee! Yippee! Hooray!

## English Sounds

- **A a** /a/: ant, alligator (audio: `/a/ /a/ /a/`, then `/a/ /a/ ant`, `/a/ /a/ alligator`)
- **B b** /b/: bat, bus (audio: `/b/ /b/ /b/`, then `/b/ /b/ bat`, `/b/ /b/ bus`)

### English Sounds Story

1. I see an ant.
2. The ant is on the bus.
3. I see a bat.
4. The bat is on the bus.
5. I see an alligator.
6. The alligator is under the bus.

## Generation Boundaries

- Use only this unit's words and sentences, plus earlier Starter units. Do not add new nouns or sentence patterns.
- Characters: Mila, Joey, Matt, Jade (see [characters](characters.md)); keep their look consistent.
- Starter learners are 5–7 years old and pre-literate: prefer listen-and-point, match, color, circle; no writing beyond tracing.
- Not used on the platform: songs, the Ditto & Fluffy comic, and the textbook listening-test track.
