# EOW3 已學總表（Spiral Digest）

用途：寫教學手冊、學習單需要「螺旋複習」時，讀這張表就知道學生在這個 Level 學過哪些單字、句型、閱讀主題，不必逐課讀全文。本表由 `unit-*.md` 的 Official Scope 自動產生，改教材請改課文檔再重跑 `tools/build_spiral_digest.py`。

| Unit | Theme | Vocabulary | Grammar | Reading | Value |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The World of Work | bus driver, chef, doctor, farmer, firefighter, nurse, police officer, scientist, vet, artist, inventor, movie star, rock star, soccer player | Simple present for occupations (What does your father do? He's a chef. / Where does he work?) · Simple present with want + infinitive (What do you want to be one day? I want to be a movie star.) | Wonderful Work! | Work hard. |
| 2 | Let's Eat! | beans, bread, corn, ice cream, mango, meat, noodles, potato, tomato, cheese, chip, grape, nut, snack, yogurt | Any in questions and negative responses (Are there any tomatoes? No, there aren't any tomatoes.) · Polite requests with may (May I have some chips, please?) | Super Snacks! | Eat good food. |
| 3 | A Helping Hand | carry, feed my pet, a goldfish, a hamster, help, hug, protect, take care of my pet, teach, come home, do my homework, have a snack, make my bed, take a shower | Time phrases with before and after (She gets dressed before breakfast. / He feeds his bird after school.) · Adverbs of frequency: never, sometimes, usually, always | My Mom, the Airplane | Take care of others. |
| 4 | My Place in the World | a bakery, a hospital, a movie theater, a museum, a park, a police station, a restaurant, a supermarket, a toy store, a train station, a library, a mall, a stadium, a swimming pool, a zoo | Ask for help with can (Can you help me? Sure. How can I help?) · Give directions with imperative + adverb (Go straight. Turn left on Third Avenue.) | Eye in the Sky | Explore your town. |
| 5 | On the Move! | an airplane, a boat, a bus, a helicopter, a motorcycle, a scooter, a ship, the subway, a taxi, downhill, get off, get on, park, uphill | Agree and disagree with too / not (I ride my scooter to school. I do, too. / I don't.) · Express contrast with but (My mother takes the bus, but my father takes the subway.) | Hot Air Balloons | Be safe on the street. |
| 6 | Our Senses | beautiful, delicious, hard, loud, quiet, soft, terrible, ugly, bitter, salty, sour, spicy, sweet | Linking verbs: taste, smell, look, feel, sound (The soup smells great.) · Simple past of to be (How was the ice cream? It was delicious. / How were the cookies?) | Amazing Animal Senses | Enjoy the world through your senses. |
| 7 | Animal Habitats | a cave, a desert, a forest, a hive, ice, an island, mud, a nest, a rain forest, snow, fur, horns, a pouch, a tongue, wings | Cause and effect with why and because (Why does a lion have sharp claws? Because it needs to catch its food.) · Infinitives of purpose (Giraffes use their long tongues to clean their ears.) | Amazing Rain Forests | Protect animal habitats. |
| 8 | What's for Dinner? | a bottle of oil, a bowl of sugar, a box of cereal, a can of soda, a glass of juice, a jar of olives, a loaf of bread, a piece of cake, buy, compare, money, a price, put away | Count and noncount nouns with some and any (Are there any oranges? Yes, there are some.) · Count and noncount nouns with a few and a little (Yes, there are a few. / There is a little.) | What I Eat | Eat nutritious food. |
