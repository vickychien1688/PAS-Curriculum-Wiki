# EOW2 已學總表（Spiral Digest）

用途：寫教學手冊、學習單需要「螺旋複習」時，讀這張表就知道學生在這個 Level 學過哪些單字、句型、閱讀主題，不必逐課讀全文。本表由 `unit-*.md` 的 Official Scope 自動產生，改教材請改課文檔再重跑 `tools/build_spiral_digest.py`。

| Unit | Theme | Vocabulary | Grammar | Reading | Value |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Animal Friends | a cat, a chicken, a cow, a dog, a duck, a goat, a horse, a sheep, a turtle, climb, crawl, fly, see, swim | Present progressive, third person plural (What are the horses doing? They're running. / Are the sheep sleeping? No, they aren't.) · Want + infinitive, 1st/2nd/3rd person singular (Do you want to ride the goat? / She wants to see the ducks.) | Animal Babies | Be good to animals. |
| 2 | Fun in Class | coloring, counting, cutting, drawing, erasing, gluing, talking, glue, a marker, a notebook, a paintbrush, scissors | Present progressive, first person plural (What are you doing? We're counting crayons.) · Are there...? / There are / There aren't (Are there any markers on the desk? No, there aren't.) | Paper Art | Be neat. |
| 3 | Boots and Bathing Suits | a bathing suit, boots, cloudy, cold, hot, a raincoat, rainy, sunny, a coat, jeans, shorts, sneakers, an umbrella | What's + noun + like? It's... (What's the weather like? It's rainy.) · Imperatives (It's cold. Put on your coat. / Don't forget your umbrella.) | Snow Animals | Dress for the weather. |
| 4 | Fun in the Sun | fly a kite, jump rope, play a game, play baseball, play basketball, play soccer, ride a bike, bounce a ball, catch a ball, play tag, throw a ball, watch a game | Like + infinitive (Do you like to play baseball? Yes, I do. It's fun. / What do you like to do?) · Let's + verb (Let's throw a ball. OK. What fun!) | A Big Ball of Fun | Be a good sport. |
| 5 | Inside Our House | a bookcase, a rug, a shower, stairs, a stove, a table, a tub, a door, a phone, a refrigerator, a sink, a window | Prepositions: above, behind, in front of, between, next to, under · Subject pronoun agreement with it/they (Where is the phone? It's in the kitchen.) | Fun Houses | Help at home. |
| 6 | Day by Day | brush my teeth, eat breakfast, eat dinner, eat lunch, get dressed, get up, go to bed, go to school, play with friends, at night, in the afternoon, in the evening, in the morning | Telling the time: exact hour (What time is it? It's 1:00. / When do you get up? At 7:00.) · Adverbs of frequency: always, every day, never | A Day in the Space Station | Be on time. |
| 7 | How Are You? | angry, bored, hungry, scared, surprised, thirsty, tired, crying, frowning, laughing, smiling, yawning | How are you? I'm + adjective; describe how a person looks (He looks bored. No, he's tired.) · Regular and irregular plurals (parent/parents, person/people, child/children) | Fabulous Faces | Be kind. |
| 8 | Awesome Animals | a giraffe, a hippo, hop, a kangaroo, a lion, a panda, a penguin, swing, a tiger, a zebra, big teeth, colorful feathers, a long trunk, sharp claws, a short tail | Express ability with can/can't (Can a penguin swim? Yes, it can. / Penguins can't fly.) · Simple present, subject-verb agreement (Does a tiger have sharp claws? Yes, it does.) | Two Big Birds | Respect animals. |
